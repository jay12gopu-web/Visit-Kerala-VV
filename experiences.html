Kerala Experiences

Eight ways to experience Kerala, with realistic locations, durations, seasons, audiences and booking guidance.

Houseboat Cruises

Float past coconut groves, paddy fields and waterside villages, with Kerala meals available on many cruises.

Best location
Alappuzha or Kumarakom
Duration
4 hours or overnight
Best season
October-February
Suitable for
Families and couples
Booking required
Yes

Hill Stations

Tea gardens, spice country, viewpoints and forest scenery create a cooler contrast to Kerala's coast.

Best location
Munnar or Wayanad
Duration
2-3 days
Best season
November-February
Suitable for
Families and nature lovers
Booking required
For guided activities

Beaches

Choose cliff views, lighthouse beaches or quieter coastal stretches, and always follow local sea conditions.

Best location
Varkala or Kovalam
Duration
Half day-2 days
Best season
November-February
Suitable for
Most travellers
Booking required
No

Ayurveda & Wellness

Choose a recognised centre, discuss health conditions honestly and confirm treatment details before booking.

Best location
Kovalam, Kumarakom or Wayanad
Duration
Consultation to multi-day stay
Best season
Year-round
Suitable for
Adults after consultation
Booking required
Yes

Arts & Festivals

Watch Kathakali, Mohiniyattam, Kutiyattam or Theyyam, with festival dates checked before travel.

Best location
Kochi, Thrissur or north Kerala
Duration
1-3 hours
Best season
Depends on the event
Suitable for
Culture-focused travellers
Booking required
Usually

Cuisine & Markets

Try appam, puttu, sadya, seafood, Malabar snacks and local produce while respecting dietary needs.

Best location
Kochi or Kozhikode
Duration
2-4 hours
Best season
Year-round
Suitable for
Food lovers and families
Booking required
Only for food tours

Wildlife & Forests

Use authorised guides, keep a safe distance from wildlife and treat sightings as never guaranteed.

Best location
Periyar or Wayanad
Duration
Half day-2 days
Best season
October-May
Suitable for
Nature lovers
Booking required
Yes

Responsible Village Life

Meet local makers, farms and community hosts through programmes that keep more tourism value local.

Best location

Duration
3 hours-full day
Best season
October-March
Suitable for
Families and student groups
