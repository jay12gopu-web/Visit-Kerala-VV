Before You Go

Travel Info

Concise, practical notes for seasons, transport, safety, health, connectivity and responsible family travel.

Quick Planning Guide

Know the essentials before choosing dates.

Conditions can change, especially during monsoon. Confirm weather, transport, opening times and provider policies close to departure.

Best Time To Visit

October to March: generally the most comfortable overall season. Coastal, hill and northern conditions can differ, so treat seasonal guidance as planning advice rather than a guarantee.

Monsoon Considerations

June to September: a greener monsoon experience with possible heavy rain. Keep flexible road plans and follow current weather and local advisories.

Warmer Months

April to May: hotter and more humid in coastal and midland areas, while hill destinations may feel comparatively cooler.

Airports

Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram and Kozhikode are the main gateways. Kannur is useful for northern Kerala routes.

Railway Access

Kerala's main towns are well connected by rail. Munnar, Thekkady and Wayanad require onward road travel.

Local Transport

Use KSRTC and private buses for value, trains for longer corridors, ferries where available and pre-booked cars for hill routes.

Languages Spoken

Malayalam is the state language. English is widely used in tourist areas; Hindi and Tamil may also be understood.

Emergency Numbers

112 is India's unified emergency number. Kerala Tourism information: 1-800-425-4747 within India.

Beach Safety

Swim only in supervised areas, follow lifeguards and warning flags, and stay out of rough seas during storms or monsoon conditions.

Boating Safety

Use recognised operators, wear a life jacket, follow crew instructions and confirm weather, age restrictions, boarding arrangements and safety equipment before departure.

Responsible Tourism

Respect local customs, ask before photographing people, avoid littering and choose community or officially classified providers where possible.

Mobile Connectivity

Mobile data and Wi-Fi are common in towns and hotels, but forest, island and hill routes can have weak patches. Save tickets and maps offline.

Health Tips

Carry regular medicines, water, insect repellent and sun protection. Consider travel insurance and seek professional medical advice for health concerns.

Family Travel

Build in rest stops, confirm child policies and life jackets, carry snacks, and avoid stacking long hill transfers with late activities.

Senior Travel

Allow slower mornings and comfort stops. Confirm steps, lifts, bathroom supports, walking distances and boat boarding directly with each provider.

What To Pack

Bring breathable clothes, walking shoes, sun protection, a light rain layer and modest clothing for religious sites.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers Before You Plan

What is the best time to visit Kerala?

October to March is generally the most comfortable overall period. June to September offers a monsoon experience, while April and May are warmer. Conditions vary by region and year.

Are the family-plan prices per person?

No. The displayed price on the regular 3-day, 5-day, 7-day and 10-day plans is an estimated total for a family of four consisting of two adults and two children.

What is included in the estimated budget?

Accommodation, local transport, meals and selected activities are included unless a plan states otherwise. The estimates are not guaranteed package prices.

Is travel to Kerala included?

Travel to and from Kerala is not included unless specifically mentioned.

Is vegetarian food easily available?

Yes. Vegetarian dishes are widely available, but travellers with allergies or strict dietary needs should confirm ingredients directly with the restaurant or accommodation.

Is Kerala suitable for children and senior citizens?

Many destinations can suit families and senior travellers when the pace and activities are chosen carefully. Confirm child rules, mobility needs, boating access and medical considerations with providers.

Which airport is best for these itineraries?

Kochi is convenient for most central and south Kerala routes. Thiruvananthapuram suits Varkala and the far south, while Kozhikode or Kannur may suit Wayanad, Bekal and northern routes.

Are the itineraries fixed?

No. They are flexible educational planning ideas and should be adjusted for weather, interests, opening times, health needs and transport availability.

Are hotel prices guaranteed?

No. Hotel ranges are indicative and can change with season, taxes, room category, availability and booking platform.

Is this an official Kerala Tourism website?

No. This is a student-created tourism exhibition project inspired by Kerala Tourism and is not an official Kerala Tourism website.
